# FIBER: Agentic Commerce Platform
## Executive Summary (One-Pager)

---

## THE IDEA IN 30 SECONDS

Fiber is your personal shopping agent that knows you deeply (your style, sizes, budget, values) and finds the best deals across 50,000+ merchants. It works with any AI agent (Claude, GPT, local LLMs, OpenClaw) and earns you cashback on every purchase.

**The vision:** Agents will make 50% of shopping decisions by 2030. Fiber is the infrastructure they'll use.

---

## THE PROBLEM

- **Agents are mainstream** but no shopping infrastructure exists
- **Amazon wins by knowing you** but optimizes for their margin, not your savings
- **Manual shopping** across merchants is impossible
- **Cashback scattered** — rewards programs people don't use

**The gap:** Agents need a standardized way to shop on behalf of users while respecting privacy and maximizing value.

---

## THE SOLUTION

### Fiber: Three Key Features

1. **Soul-Based Personalization**
   - Store your preferences (SOUL.md), never shared without consent
   - Your agent learns your taste, size, style, budget over time
   - Better recommendations = higher conversion

2. **Multi-Merchant Search**
   - Search ALL merchants simultaneously (50,000+)
   - One-line comparison: price, cashback, merchant
   - Best deal highlighted automatically

3. **Flexible Cashback**
   - **Option A (Simple):** Cashback stays on Fiber, use it to shop again
   - **Option B (Crypto):** Get MON tokens in your MetaMask, hold or trade

### Interoperability
Works with any agent:
- OpenClaw (local)
- Claude, GPT (cloud)
- Llama, Falcon (open-source)
- Future agents TBD

## BUSINESS MODEL

**Revenue:** 2% affiliate commission per purchase
- Merchants pay only when customers convert
- We only send pre-qualified buyers
- Win-win (they get customers, we get commission)

**Example:** 
- 100K users × 2.5 purchases/month × $150 AOV = $37.5M GMV/month
- 2% commission = $750K/month = $9M/year
