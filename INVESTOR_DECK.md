## 2. THE VISION: AGENTIC COMMERCE

### What is Agentic Commerce?
Commerce powered by AI agents that know YOU personally—your taste, size, budget, values—and make decisions on your behalf.

### Two Futures Converge

**Future 1: Personal Agents (3+ years)**
- Everyone runs their own AI agent (OpenClaw, local LLM, etc)
- Knows everything about you (your "soul")
- Makes all shopping decisions independently
- Full privacy, full control

**Future 2: Fiber Cloud Agents (Today)**
- You register on Fiber
- We store your preferences, personality, taste profile
- Access your agent from anywhere
- Simpler, immediate, for 99% of users

**The Vision:** These two converge. Whether you run agents locally or on Fiber, they know you deeply.

**Q: Why not just improve Amazon?**
A: Amazon optimizes for their margin, not your savings. We optimize for agents—a new category.

**Q: What if Amazon builds this?**
A: They're monolithic. We're protocol-first. Agents will choose best deals, not Amazon. We'll be default.

**Q: Crypto is risky. Why include MON tokens?**
A: Hedged approach. 80% users use cloud cashback (simple). 20% opt into crypto. Best of both worlds.

**Q: Who's your competition?**
A: Nobody yet. By the time competitors realize, we'll have network effects + 1M users.

**Q: What if people don't use agents for shopping?**
A: Even if agents are 10% of shopping, $5.3T × 10% = $530B TAM. Still huge.

**Q: How do you get merchants?**
A: Affiliate fees (we pay them for conversions). Same model as Rakuten, but better UX. Win-win.
