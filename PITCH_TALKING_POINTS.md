# Fiber Pitch Presentation
## Talking Points & Narrative Flow

---

## OPENING (1 minute)

### The Hook
"In 5 years, AI agents will make most shopping decisions. Nobody's built the infrastructure for that yet. We're building it."

### The Pain Point
- You love deals, but manually searching across 50K stores is impossible
- Amazon knows you but optimizes for their profit, not your savings
- Cashback scattered across apps you don't use
- **The real problem:** There's no "personal shopping agent" that actually knows you

---

## ACT 1: THE VISION (2 minutes)

### Story Setup
"Imagine your shopping assistant knows everything about you:
- Your style (minimalist? trendy? classic?)
- Your sizes (shoe 10, shirt M, jeans 32)
- Your budget ($50–200 per item)
- Your values (sustainable? brand-loyal? price-hunter?)
- Your history (what you loved, what you returned)

Now imagine that assistant searches all merchants simultaneously and finds the best deal that matches YOU."

### Two Paths Converge
**Long-term (3+ years):** People run their own agents locally (OpenClaw on home servers)
**Short-term (TODAY):** We store their "agent soul" in Fiber cloud—same experience, no hardware

### Why This Matters
"This is the shift from 'algorithmic commerce' (Amazon decides for you) to 'agentic commerce' (your agent decides for you)."

---

## ACT 2: WHAT WE'RE BUILDING (3 minutes)

### Demo or Walkthrough
"Let me show you Fiber in action..."

**Scenario:** User searches "winter boots under $150"

1. **Agent understands context**
   - "I'm a size 8, prefer minimal design, care about durability"
   
2. **Searches all merchants**
   - Nike, Adidas, REI, Target, Amazon, Finish Line, Shopify stores...
   
3. **Returns personalized results**
   - Best 3 matches (sorted by "fit for you" not just price)
   - Price comparison table (Nike vs Adidas vs Target)
   - Cashback highlighted
   
4. **One-click purchase**
   - Redirects to merchant, earns you cashback

### Key Features
1. **Soul Storage**
   - Your preferences encrypted in Fiber
   - Grows smarter with time
   - Only you + your agents access it

2. **Cashback Model (Choose Yours)**
   - **Simple:** Cashback stays on Fiber, use it to shop again
   - **Crypto:** Get MON tokens in your MetaMask, trade or hold

3. **Works with ANY agent**
   - OpenClaw running on your Mac? Fiber works.
   - Claude? Gemini? Open-source Llama? All work.

## HANDLING OBJECTIONS

### "Why not just improve Amazon's recommendations?"
**Response:** "Amazon's AI optimizes for their margin, not your savings. It's a fundamental conflict of interest. We optimize for agents—a completely new category that doesn't exist yet."

### "What if Amazon/Google builds this?"
**Response:** "They're monolithic, slow to move. By the time they build it, we'll have 1M users + merchant network effects. Plus, agents won't use Amazon's version—they'll use the best deal aggregator (us). It's winner-take-most."

### "Crypto is risky. The cashback feature scares traditional users."
**Response:** "We hedge both bets. 80% of users get simple cloud cashback (zero crypto). 20% opt into MON tokens. We don't force Web3 on anyone, but we capture early adopters who want it. Best of both worlds."

### "Who's your competition?"
**Response:** "Right now? Nobody. Rakuten is closest but they're mobile-app focused. Cashback sites are 2010. Nobody is building agent-native shopping. By the time competitors realize, we'll own the protocol layer and have network effects."

### "How do you get merchants to integrate?"
**Response:** "Same model as Rakuten. We drive customers to them (which Amazon doesn't do). Merchants pay affiliate fees—but it's worth it because:
1. We only send serious buyers (pre-qualified by agent)
2. Conversion rate is higher (personalized matches)
3. Customer lifetime value higher (better recommendations)"

### "What if agents never become mainstream for shopping?"
**Response:** "Even if 10% of shopping goes through agents, that's $530B TAM. Still enormous. Plus, we work for humans too—anyone can use Fiber directly. We're hedged."

### "Why not just build a ChatGPT plugin?"
**Response:** "Plugins are toy projects. We're building infrastructure. ChatGPT is a client, we're the ecosystem. When 1000 different agents compete for shopping, Fiber becomes the standard layer they all use."

### "What's your defensibility? Can't anyone copy this?"
**Response:** "
1. **Network effects:** Merchants integrate once, stay because of volume
2. **Data moat:** User preferences + purchase history
3. **Agent ecosystem:** Early partnerships with OpenClaw, etc.
4. **Protocol ownership:** We define the MCP standards for shopping
5. **Speed:** 6–12 month head start is huge in this market

By the time someone copies, we'll have 1M users and merchants won't move."

## PRESENTATION TIPS

1. **Lead with vision, not product**
   - "Shopping is moving to agents" (big idea)
   - Not "We built a chatbot" (boring)

2. **Use storytelling, not slides**
   - "Imagine your agent knows you..." (visual)
   - Not "Our product has 5 features" (dry)

3. **Demo > Slides**
   - If possible, show actual Fiber in action
   - Search, compare, see cashback
   - Investors want to see it working

4. **Emphasize timing**
   - Agents are mainstream NOW (2024)
   - This is the right window
   - "6–12 months before competitors move"

5. **End with vision, not ask**
   - Close with: "We're building the infrastructure for how humans shop in 2030"
   - Then ask: "Who wants to be part of that?"

6. **Practice the pitch**
   - 20 min presentation
   - 10 min Q&A
   - Rehearse objection handling
